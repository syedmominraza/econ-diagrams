    How to use it

    Built for a quick review after you have revised from your class notes.

    1. Find the spec point

      Open a theme below, pick the spec point, and jump straight to the notes for it.

    2. Watch it move

      Click through the buttons. Each one redraws the diagram and explains it as a chain of analysis with an evaluation point.

    3. Draw it yourself

      On the practice page, build the diagram from the pieces, then reveal the answer and tick off what you got right.

    Spec index

    Every section of Themes 3 and 4, and the major parts of Themes 1 and 2. Open a section to see its spec points, click a spec point to jump to its notes, or use "What's covered" to pick a specific note.

    About this site

    Slaude Economics was built by Syed, a Year 13 student, with the AI assistant Claude. The diagrams follow the shapes and labels from our class handouts, and the notes are written as chains of analysis with an evaluation point, because that is where the marks are.

    It is a revision aid, not a textbook. For top marks you still need your class notes and past papers.

    Good to know

    • Most diagrams have no numbers, so the labels match what you draw in the exam.
    • The real-world examples are kept free of statistics, which go out of date.
    • If something looks wrong, check it with your teacher.
